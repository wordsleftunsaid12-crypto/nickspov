## Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Dark, cinematic, intimate. The design should feel like stepping into someone's perspective — personal, raw, authentic. No corporate polish. The aesthetic is closer to a premium adult platform than a SaaS site.

**Mood:** Confidential, voyeuristic, premium but not pretentious
**Density:** Medium — enough breathing room to feel premium, but not sparse
**Motion:** Subtle — fade-ins on scroll, hover states on cards, smooth transitions

## Color Palette & Roles

- **Background:** #0a0a0a (near black)
- **Surface:** #141414 (cards, nav)
- **Border:** #2a2a2a (subtle separation)
- **Text Primary:** #ffffff
- **Text Secondary:** #a0a0a0
- **Accent:** #e91e63 (pink/red — CTAs, highlights)
- **Accent Hover:** #f06292

## Typography Rules

- **Headlines:** System font stack, bold, tight letter-spacing
- **Body:** System font stack, regular weight
- **Scale:** 3rem (hero) → 1.75rem (section) → 1.1rem (card) → 0.875rem (caption)
- **Line height:** 1.6 for body, 1.2 for headlines

## Component Stylings

- **Buttons:** 8px radius, primary (accent) or secondary (surface with border)
- **Cards:** 12px radius, 1px border, subtle hover lift
- **Nav:** Sticky, blur backdrop, minimal
- **Forms:** 8px radius, dark inputs, clear focus states

## Layout Principles

- Max width: 1200px
- Grid: responsive, 3 columns on desktop → 1 on mobile
- Spacing scale: 0.5rem, 1rem, 1.5rem, 2rem, 3rem, 4rem
- Sections separated by 3-4rem padding

## Depth & Elevation

- Cards: 1px border, no shadow (flat design)
- Age gate: full overlay with backdrop blur
- Nav: subtle backdrop blur

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do:** Use the accent color sparingly (CTAs only)
- **Do:** Keep the design dark and moody
- **Do:** Make it feel premium and exclusive
- **Don't:** Use gradients (except subtle text gradients on hero)
- **Don't:** Add unnecessary decorations
- **Don't:** Use em dashes
- **Don't:** Make it look like a template

## Responsive Behavior

- Mobile-first approach
- Grid collapses to single column
- Nav collapses to hamburger on mobile (optional)
- Touch targets minimum 44px
